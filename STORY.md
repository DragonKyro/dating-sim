# STORY.md — Last Ferry

The story bible. Level JSON (`src/content/levels/`) and persona prompts (`src/content/prompts/personas/`)
are the source of truth for what plays; this file explains *why*, so new
content stays consistent with it.

---

## Logline

You have one summer on a small island where everyone listens to the same
pirate radio show at 2 a.m. When the last ferry of the season leaves on
Lantern Night, who will you be standing next to?

## The engine of the story: stay or go

Saltwick Island triples in population from June to August, then empties. The
ferry *Persistence* runs four times a day until **Lantern Night (August 31)**,
when the last summer ferry leaves at midnight and the island drops to a winter
schedule of two boats a week, weather permitting.

Everyone in the cast is deciding whether to stay or leave — and so are you,
because your summer ends that night too.

| Character | Wants | Afraid of | The choice |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rosie | to leave (a pastry school in Lyon) | abandoning her grandmother's bakery | letting herself want something |
| Noor | to stay, and be known here | being seen and found lacking | dropping the anonymous voice |
| Tamsin | to stay somewhere, once | being missed / missing anyone | letting a place (and a person) keep her |
| Celeste | to stay (the real, off-season island) | everyone's expectations of a Whitlock | staying on her own terms |

**Tone:** warm, funny, specific, a little end-of-summer melancholy. Romance
through *attention*: the game rewards noticing people. Every secret is a
**want** someone is hiding, not a wound for the player to fix. Nobody needs
rescuing; they need someone who listens well enough to be told.

## You

A blank slate. You're house-sitting **Gull Cottage** for your great-aunt
**Bea Halloran** (81), who is on a cruise around Iceland "with a man named
Teodoro." You arrive on the 4:10 ferry on Saturday, July 26. Why you needed
somewhere to be this summer is yours to decide — characters may ask, and the
player answers however they like. The game remembers what you tell people
(see "Memory" below).

Characters refer to you by the name and pronouns set in Settings.

## The island

- **Saltwick** — one main street (Front Street), one wharf, one of everything.
  Gray shingles, hydrangeas, a fog that arrives like a rumor.
- **The ferry *Persistence*** — Captain **Ozzie Pires** (70) announces
  departures with improvised poetry.
- **Amaral's Bakery** — Front Street, opens at 6, smells like it from 3.
  Malasadas, sweet bread, pastéis de nata. Fifty years old.
- **Second Spin** — Wharf Street. Records and radio repair in a crooked
  building. The flat upstairs is rented to Tamsin.
- **The North Light** — decommissioned lighthouse on the north point. At its
  base is an old mailbox where islanders drop anonymous letters for…
- **Low Tide FM, 88.1** — an unlicensed broadcast every night at 2 a.m. "for
  the insomniacs, the night shift, and the lonely." The host reads the
  letters on air: apologies, confessions, gossip, a proposal or two. The voice
  is filtered; nobody knows who it is. Council chair **Dot Kettleby** has
  vowed to find out. (It's Noor, broadcasting from the North Light's lamp
  room.)
- **The Saltwick Yacht Club** — summer people, a terrace with string lights.
- **Highwater** — the Whitlock family's shingled house on the north bluff,
  four generations old, sold this summer. Its boathouse holds *Marguerite*.
- **The seal ledges** — off the east headland. 214 grey seals, by Tamsin's
  count.

## The cast

### Rosie Amaral — 23 — *all momentum*
Runs **Amaral's Bakery**, founded by her grandmother **Vó Lurdes** (84), who
had a stroke in March. Rosie has run the 4 a.m. shifts alone since. Loud,
quick, talks with her hands, nicknames everyone, burns herself constantly,
turns everything into a bit. Island kid through and through.
- **Secret:** an acceptance letter from a pâtisserie school in **Lyon**,
  pinned under a magnet on the bakery fridge. Starts September 15. She's
  deferred once already. She hasn't told her mother or Vó.
- **Tests:** energy and play. Say yes to her bits, keep up, be fun — then
  notice the tiredness under the cheer.
- **Arc:** permission to want something for herself.

### Noor Haddad — 25 — *the person who notices*
Runs **Second Spin**. Lebanese-American, came to the island three years ago
from the city, where she was a mixing engineer making other people's records
sound perfect until she forgot what she liked. Dry, precise, terrible at
small talk, brilliant at listening. Answers questions with better questions.
- **Secret:** she's the voice of **Low Tide FM**. People tell "the radio"
  things they'd never tell her face to face, and that's the only way she's
  let herself be close to anyone here.
- **Tests:** listening. Specific questions, comfortable silence, honesty about
  what you don't know. Performative charm bounces off.
- **Arc:** from anonymous voice to being seen.

### Tamsin Oyelaran — 27 — *always leaving*
British marine biologist on a three-month grey seal survey. Blunt, practical,
bone-dry, allergic to tourists who ask if she swims with them. Wears a yellow
slicker in any weather. Rents the flat above Second Spin.
- **Secret:** she has named all 214 seals (the bull is Gerald; a pup called
  Biscuit is her unprofessional favorite). Bigger secret: she's taking
  fourteen months at an **Antarctic research station**, leaving in October.
  Her mum moved them eleven times before she was fifteen; she learned to
  love places in advance so leaving wouldn't hurt.
- **Tests:** competence and curiosity. Be useful, ask about the work, laugh at
  yourself, don't whine, don't waste her time.
- **Arc:** letting somewhere — someone — keep her.

### Celeste Whitlock — 24 — *the game she's always winning*
A summer person: the Whitlocks have owned **Highwater** for four generations.
Polished, cutting, plays every conversation like a match she intends to win.
Bored of the yacht club. Tests everyone.
- **Secret:** the family is **selling Highwater**; it closes September 1.
  She's secretly restoring her grandfather Win's 1962 wooden sloop
  ***Marguerite*** to sail it once on Lantern Night before it goes with the
  house. Deeper: she's been asking the boatyard for a winter job. She wants to
  stay on the real island, the off-season one.
- **Tests:** sparring. Hold your own, call her bluff with good humor, don't be
  impressed by money, never flatter.
- **Arc:** dropping the performance; staying on her own terms.

### Supporting cast (narration and dialogue only, no sprites)
- **Great-Aunt Bea** — absent; her handwritten notes cover the cottage
  ("THE KETTLE LIES. It says it's off. It is never off.").
- **Captain Ozzie Pires** — ferry captain, poet of departures.
- **Vó Lurdes Amaral** — formidable, critiques the bread from her armchair.
- **Dot Kettleby** — council chair, power-walker, hunting Low Tide FM; suspects
  the newcomer (you).
- **Hugo Whitlock** — Celeste's older brother, handling the sale. Practical,
  not a villain.
- **Pip** — a three-legged dog who belongs to nobody and turns up everywhere.

### Connections the player can discover
- **Rosie ↔ Celeste:** best friends every summer from age 8 to 14 (forts on the
  bluff). Celeste went to boarding school and stopped coming for two summers;
  Rosie started working. Each thinks the other one let go first.
- **Noor ↔ Tamsin:** landlady and tenant, midnight tea. Tamsin: "My landlady
  keeps the strangest hours."
- **Everyone ↔ Low Tide FM:** Tamsin phones in anonymous seal-census updates.
  Rosie once wrote in about "a secret in an envelope." Celeste wrote in about a
  house. Noor read every letter.

A full playthrough is 10 scenes: four meetings, up to four second
meetings, one storm, one finale.

### Endings
- **Together** (one per character): reached by winning their finale.
- **Bittersweet** (one per character): losing the finale. You board the last
  ferry alone and Low Tide FM plays you out. Replayable.

## Mechanics the story leans on

- **Interest (in-scene, 0–100):** how into *this* conversation she is. Starts
  at the level's `startInterest` (plus a small bonus from your bond), moves
  every turn by the judge's score. Hit 0 and she leaves.
- **Affection (lasting bond, 0–100):** changes only when a scene ends.
  Unlocks nothing directly; it nudges later scenes' starting interest.
- **Objectives:** `reach` (get her interest to the bar), `ask` (make the move
  yourself at the right moment — too early and she deflects, too many times
  and she's done), `endure` (keep her above a floor until the scene ends).
- **Secrets:** guarded topics. Probe one once she trusts you enough and she
  tells you (sets a story flag and becomes a memory); too early and she
  deflects. Secrets carry forward even if you lose the scene.
- **Memory:** each scene leaves the character an authored memory of what
  happened, plus up to three facts she learned about you, pulled from what
  you actually said. Later scenes put these in her prompt, so she remembers.
- **Flavor cards:** each level opens with one of Bea's notes or a Low Tide FM
  transcript, with variants keyed to story flags so the island reacts to
  what you've done.

## Writing rules for new content

- **Specific beats generic.** Not "a coffee shop" — "4 a.m., one oven
  ticking, flour on the radio dial, the fishermen due at six."
- **Write the deflections.** Every ask objective needs a `refuse` line in the
  character's voice; every secret needs a trigger the player can plausibly
  hit from the scene's context.
- **Give the player handles.** The `situation` should put two or three
  concrete things in the scene she can be asked about. Tips in the briefing
  should hint at what she likes without giving away the secret.
- **Wants, not wounds.** No trauma reveals as rewards. Vulnerability is
  welcome; suffering as a prize is not.
- **One speaker per scene.** Others appear in narration and dialogue
  references only (multi-speaker scenes are future work).
