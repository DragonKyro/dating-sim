# STORY.md — Story Outline (v1 Draft)

A first-pass sketch of the narrative spine: who, where, when, how it all
collides. Everything here is a *starting point*, not a commitment. Pick what
works, throw out what doesn't.

For mechanics brainstorm (settings, personalities, objectives) see
[DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md). For per-level configs see [levels/](levels/).

---

## Premise

It's **fall semester at State University**. The MC ("you") is a **20-year-old
sophomore**, undeclared. The MC has **no fixed personality** — they're a
blank slate the player projects into. No backstory, no defined hometown, no
band-from-high-school. The player's voice IS the MC's voice. We don't
overwrite that with authored history. (This is a deliberate design call:
the writing reacts to the player rather than performing alongside them.)

The MC lives in an off-campus apartment with one roommate, **Marcus**.

The story spans roughly **September through December** — about 12–14 weeks
of in-game time, with each level taking place on a specific day. You meet
several girls in the first few weeks; how things develop from there depends
on which threads you pull.

The point isn't to "win all five." The point is to figure out who *you* are
by paying close attention to who *they* are.

## Main Characters (the five girls)

Each character has: a personality you can read in 30 seconds, a backstory
that unfolds over multiple levels, and a relationship arc that goes wrong
in a different way than the others.

### 1. Emma — *the easy one to like, harder to keep*

- **Age 20, Communications major, sophomore.** Sits next to you in Psych 101.
- **Surface:** Friendly, social, listens to true crime podcasts, gets coffee
  with friends, normal-girl-next-door. The first impression is "she seems
  really nice."
- **First meeting:** Level 01 — after Psych 101 lecture. ✓ exists.
- **What's beneath:**
  - Has a reputation as "the dependable one" in her friend group — the one
    who organizes group plans, the one everyone vents to.
  - Quietly worried she's *the friend, not the girlfriend*. Got ghosted six
    months ago by a guy she'd been seeing for three months (he just
    stopped texting back, no fight, no explanation). It bruised her
    confidence in a way she doesn't openly admit.
  - Has a best friend, **Hannah** (more on her below), who is fiercely
    protective and will scrutinize you.
- **Reveal sequence (across levels):**
  - L1: She's a normal classmate. No backstory yet.
  - L~3-4: She mentions she "had a thing this summer that fizzled."
  - L~6-7: She tells you about Hannah, and that Hannah's opinion of you
    matters. Hannah event triggers.
  - L~9-10: The full ghosting story comes out, in a quiet moment. This is
    the vulnerability beat that flips her from "I like talking to you" to
    "I'm letting you in."
- **What ends her arc badly:** Treating her like an easy win. Being smooth.
  Talking to her the same way you'd talk to anyone else.
- **What ends her arc well:** Showing up consistently. Being curious about
  her *life*, not her status as a girl-you-could-date. Earning Hannah.

### 2. Mira — *the locked door with the good music behind it*

- **Age 21, English Literature major, junior.** Quiet, reads on every
  surface, lives in the library.
- **Surface:** Introverted, soft-spoken, smart, dismissive of small talk.
  Reads philosophy, drinks black coffee, takes margin notes in pencil.
  Looks like she's mildly annoyed even when she's not.
- **First meeting:** A planned future level — university library, finals
  week, you end up at the same study table by accident and she keeps
  glancing at the book you're failing to read.
- **What's beneath:**
  - Only child of academic parents (one professor, one researcher). Was
    raised on the idea that emotion is a kind of weakness — feelings are
    fine as long as you can also analyze them away.
  - Writes poetry. Has notebooks she's shown no one. The poetry is *good*,
    and she knows it, but she's terrified of being seen as the cliché
    "poet girl in lit class."
  - Has a complicated friendship with Yuki (see below) — they're each
    other's only "real" friend even though they're nothing alike.
- **Reveal sequence:**
  - First meeting: barely-there small talk. She doesn't open up easily.
  - Early-mid: she mentions her parents in a deflecting joke — first
    crack in the wall.
  - Mid: you catch her writing in a notebook and she immediately closes
    it. Don't push.
  - Late: she reads you a poem (one she wrote). This is the largest
    vulnerability moment in the entire game for any character.
- **What ends her arc badly:** Pretending to know books you haven't read.
  Trying to "save" her or fix her. Being loud where she needs quiet.
- **What ends her arc well:** Patience. Honesty about your own gaps.
  Earning the right to be told about the notebook.

### 3. June — *the wildfire with a backstory*

- **Age 22, Studio Art major, senior.** Lives in chaotic mode at all times.
  Probably the most magnetic person you've met.
- **Surface:** Vivid, intense, unpredictable. Wears mismatched things on
  purpose. Talks fast, laughs loud, knows everyone in the art building.
  Drinks too much coffee.
- **First meeting:** A planned future level — gallery opening night for the
  student art showcase. She's exhibiting. You overhear her arguing with
  someone about her own piece and stick around.
- **What's beneath:**
  - Dated **Owen** (musician, kind of locally famous in a small-time way)
    for two years. He was charismatic and controlling — the kind of
    relationship friends saw from outside and were uneasy about. She left
    six months ago. He's back in town this semester.
  - Her art has become her therapy. The piece in the showcase is *about
    Owen* — she knows it, the people who know her know it, Owen knows it.
  - Used to be best friends with Yuki in high school. They had a falling
    out junior year over something Yuki said about Owen at the time. It's
    never been resolved.
- **Reveal sequence:**
  - First meeting: she's chaotic and charming. You don't get the backstory.
  - Mid: she mentions an ex in passing, makes a joke, deflects.
  - Owen event: Owen shows up at a party / her show / a coffee shop and
    interacts with you. You learn who he is by how she reacts.
  - Late: she tells you the real story of the breakup, in a place that
    matters (her studio, a park bench at 2am, etc.).
  - Latest: the Yuki connection comes out, possibly through Yuki.
- **What ends her arc badly:** Trying to play it cool when Owen shows up.
  Letting her self-destruct without saying anything. Treating her chaos
  as performance instead of a coping mechanism.
- **What ends her arc well:** Being steady when she isn't. Telling her
  when something feels off, even when she'll be mad. Not asking her to
  perform "happy."

### 4. Yuki — *the one who would rather break than bend*

- **Age 20, pre-med, junior (accelerated).** Cold-professional vibe with
  a side of confident-extrovert. You feel slightly underdressed near her.
- **Surface:** Composed, efficient, doesn't waste words. Will absolutely
  make eye contact while you're trying to figure out what to say. Books
  her gym sessions in advance.
- **First meeting:** A planned future level — possibly the gym, possibly
  a coffee shop where she's studying, possibly a wedding reception where
  she's a +1. Setting still TBD.
- **What's beneath:**
  - Parents are both doctors. Older brother is also pre-med (now at
    Hopkins). The expectation that she perform was set before she could
    talk. She has never told her family she gets panic attacks.
  - Performs confidence as armor. Genuinely *is* confident in a lot of
    domains; it's the high-stakes emotional ones where the armor is
    holding back something that would otherwise collapse her.
  - Friends with Mira because Mira doesn't perform around her — they
    have the rare ability to sit in silence together without it being
    weird.
  - Was friends with June in high school. The falling out (see June)
    matters more to her than she lets on.
- **Reveal sequence:**
  - First meeting: she's intimidating but engaging. You might not even
    realize you "won" the encounter until it's over.
  - Early-mid: she mentions her brother. The pressure beneath the words
    is visible if you're paying attention.
  - Mid: you see her crack briefly — a missed answer in front of
    classmates, a moment alone on a stairwell, a phone call from her
    mom she abruptly ends.
  - Late: she has a real moment with you. This isn't a poetry reading
    or a confession; it's quieter. She tells you she's been having
    panic attacks. She has not told her family. She has not told her
    brother. She is telling *you*.
- **What ends her arc badly:** Being intimidated. Being too eager. Trying
  to be "the guy who fixes her." Looking surprised when she shows you
  she's tired.
- **What ends her arc well:** Treating her like a peer, not a prize.
  Being matter-of-fact when she shows you a crack — no fawning, no
  performative sympathy, just *being there*.

### 5. Naia — *the one who knows everyone but is known by almost no one*

- **Age 20, undeclared (leaning Marketing), sophomore.** Works 25+ hours a
  week at **Common Grounds**, the campus coffee shop.
- **Surface:** Warm, social, remembers your name and drink order after one
  visit. The kind of optimism that comes from "I've already seen worse,"
  not from naivety. Quick laugh. Sharper than she lets on. Almost every
  Common Grounds regular has had a real conversation with her across the
  counter at some point.
- **First meeting:** A planned future level — Common Grounds coffee shop,
  any time you'd realistically stop in. Quiet shift, no line. She makes
  conversation while making your drink. You leave realizing she got more
  out of you than you got out of her.
- **What's beneath:**
  - First-generation college student on partial scholarship. Older sister
    works back home; younger brother is still in high school. Naia sends
    most of her tip money home. Has told no one in the campus social
    scene any of this.
  - The friendliness is real. So is the watchfulness. You don't survive
    being first-gen at a state school full of comparatively rich kids
    without learning to read every room you enter.
  - She knows almost the whole cast through the coffee shop: Emma, Mira,
    Hannah, and Yuki are regulars; June drops in occasionally; **Marcus**
    asked her out freshman year and she shut it down without softening
    the landing; **Owen** asked her out once between June and someone
    else, and she said no — which means Owen knows her, and she has
    opinions about him from outside June's bubble.
- **Reveal sequence:**
  - First meeting: she's a friendly barista. You don't get the depth.
  - Early-mid: she lets one specific real thing slip — a detail about
    her hometown, her sister, the cost of her textbooks — then deflects.
    You decide whether to remember it.
  - Mid: she tells you who you'd see in the cast if you watched them from
    behind a counter. Her insights on other girls become available *if
    you've earned them*. These aren't free hints; they advance her arc
    too.
  - Late: the actual story — the money, the sister, why she's working so
    many shifts. Not delivered as a confession. Delivered as: "you know
    enough now that I'm tired of hiding it."
- **What ends her arc badly:** Mistaking the friendliness for a signal.
  Trying to "rescue" her. Treating her financial situation as a problem
  *you* need to solve. Being condescending without realizing it.
- **What ends her arc well:** Earning the difference between her customer
  voice and her real voice. Showing up at her place / her work for reasons
  that aren't about you. Treating her as competent rather than burdened.

## Supporting Cast

These characters don't get their own levels (probably), but they show up
inside other characters' levels and shape the story.

### Marcus — *your roommate, your accidental love rival*

- **Age 21, business major, junior.** Charismatic, easy with people,
  player.
- Doesn't realize he's a rival. He's not malicious — he's just *also*
  charming, and he meets people through living with you.
- He genuinely likes you. Will hit on Emma without thinking about it.
  Will hit on Yuki even though Yuki is way out of his depth. Won't go
  near Mira (he doesn't know how to talk to her) or June (she'd eat him).
- The MC will face moments of: "Marcus is talking to Emma at a party.
  What do you do?" The answer isn't always "intervene."
- Backstory beat: Marcus has a sister who got cheated on. He's a player
  but he draws a hard line at *cheating*. If the MC tries to date two
  girls who both know each other, Marcus is the one who'll call you out.

### Hannah — *Emma's best friend, your judge*

- **Age 20, Comm major, Emma's roommate.** Sharp, observant, fiercely
  loyal to Emma.
- Shows up in mid-game Emma levels. The "Hannah judges you" event
  determines whether your Emma path stays open. Pass = Emma starts
  trusting you more. Fail = Hannah subtly pushes Emma away from you.
- Hannah is not a romance option. She's the *gatekeeper*.

### Owen — *June's controlling ex*

- **Age 24, local musician.** Charming on first impression. Watch his
  face when June laughs at something you said.
- Shows up at events June is at — a gallery opening, a show, a party.
  Will be polite to you while making sure June feels surveilled.
- The Owen confrontation level (late game) is one of the highest-stakes
  moments — say the wrong thing and you blow June's whole arc. Say the
  right thing and you cement it.

### Ethan — *Yuki's pre-med study partner*

- **Age 21, pre-med, junior.** The substantive rival for Yuki.
- Where Marcus is shallow charm, Ethan is the real thing — top of his
  bio class, polite, well-dressed, will-shake-your-hand-on-meeting.
- Yuki sees him every week in study group. He's already won the
  *competence* battle the MC has to fight without trying to look like
  he's fighting it.
- Plot use: a mid-game level where Yuki mentions she's grabbing coffee
  with Ethan to "go over orgo." Not a date — except it kind of is. MC
  decides whether to make it a problem.
- Not malicious. Genuinely likes Yuki. Genuinely *would* be a good
  match for her if MC isn't paying attention.

### Caleb — *Mira's TA, intellectual rival*

- **Age 25, grad student in English literature, TA for one of Mira's
  classes.** Older, intellectually serious, takes her work seriously
  in a way most people don't.
- Engages with Mira on the exact thing she'd never share with the MC
  unprompted — her writing. Reads her papers and writes margin
  comments she actually treasures.
- Plot use: Mira mentions a "really helpful office hour" once and the
  MC realizes who she's talking about. The threat isn't romantic in a
  pushy way; it's that he's the only adult who's *seen the poetry*.
- The slow-burn rival. Doesn't show up until mid-game.

### Tyler — *Naia's persistent customer*

- **Age 22, business major, senior.** Comes to Common Grounds every
  weekday afternoon and has been low-key pursuing Naia for months.
- Not predatory — just persistent in a way Naia has gotten good at
  managing. He's the comic-relief rival: present in the background of
  most Naia coffee-shop scenes, gives the MC something to navigate
  around without it being high-stakes.
- Plot use: when Naia's affection with MC is rising, Tyler escalates
  one notch. MC chooses how/whether to intervene. (Naia is fully
  capable of handling him; intervening unasked is the wrong move.)

### Hannah's Boyfriend / Daniel — *cameo only*

- Daniel is Hannah's long-term boyfriend. He's nice. He's irrelevant.
  He's mainly a flag for the player: "Hannah is taken, she's just here
  to evaluate you." Prevents the player from misreading her sharpness
  as flirtation.

### Tomo — *Yuki's older brother (cameo only, phone calls)*

- Never appears on-screen. Mentioned by Yuki, sometimes calls her mid-
  scene. The shadow of him is the shape of half her pressure.

**Key adjacencies the player will discover:**

*Friendship/social network:*
- **Emma + Hannah**: best friends. Hannah will appear in Emma's mid-game arc.
- **Mira + Yuki**: close friends despite being opposites. If you're close
  with one, the other will hear about you.
- **Naia** knows **everyone** through Common Grounds: Emma, Mira, Hannah, and
  Yuki are regulars; June drops in occasionally. Naia is the social-network
  hub from a position outside the friend groups.

*Past tensions:*
- **Yuki + June**: ex-friends, fallout in high school over Owen. If June's
  arc unlocks, Yuki has feelings about it whether she shows them or not.
- **Marcus + Naia**: Marcus asked Naia out freshman year and got a flat no.
  No bad blood, but he avoids the shop, and Naia has been reading him as
  shallow ever since.
- **Owen + Naia**: Owen tried to date her between June and someone else.
  She said no. Owen remembers; Naia doesn't.

*Rival-related:*
- **Marcus + Emma**: Marcus will independently flirt with Emma.
  Direct triangle rival.
- **Owen + June**: Owen is the toxic ex that haunts June's arc.
- **Ethan + Yuki**: pre-med study partner; competence rival.
- **Caleb + Mira**: her TA who reads her writing; intellectual rival.
- **Tyler + Naia**: persistent customer; soft comic-relief rival.

*Peripheral:*
- Emma + Mira: peripheral acquaintances (same English class). Not friends,
  but they'd recognize each other.
- Daniel (Hannah's BF): cameo only.
- Tomo (Yuki's brother): off-screen phone calls only.

## Story Arc (four acts, ~12 weeks)

### Act 1 — Meet (weeks 1–3, ~5 levels)

The five girls enter the MC's life. Each introduction is a self-contained
"first impression" scenario.

- **L01 — After Psych 101**: Emma. ✓ exists.
- **L02 — Library, finals-week-vibes**: Mira. First chance encounter,
  high friction.
- **L03 — Gallery opening**: June. Loud first impression, you're flagged
  but not committed.
- **L04 — TBD setting**: Yuki. Could be gym, coffee shop, or wedding
  reception (placeholder). The hardest first encounter.
- **L05 — Common Grounds, quiet shift**: Naia. The "easy" first
  encounter — she'll talk to anyone — but the depth doesn't unlock
  yet. Acts as a tutorial-paced reintroduction late in Act 1.

Acts 1 levels are gated only by *order* (do them in any sequence). The
objective in each is small: get a number, get an Instagram, schedule a
hangout, exchange book titles, etc.

### Act 2 — Develop (weeks 4–7, ~6–8 levels)

Second meetings. Backstory cracks start showing. Supporting cast appears.

- Second-meet with each girl in a different setting (coffee shop with
  Emma; reading nook with Mira; her studio with June; campus walk with
  Yuki; late-night Common Grounds with Naia closing the shop).
- **The Hannah Event**: Hannah corners you to evaluate. Pass affects
  Emma path.
- **Marcus moments**: at a party / in your apartment, Marcus mentions
  he's "thinking about asking Emma for coffee." Player chooses to
  redirect or stay silent. (Affects Emma; affects Marcus's later
  willingness to back off.)
- **Owen sighting** (if June arc is active): you see Owen at one of
  June's events. Don't engage; she explains later.
- **Yuki's first crack**: you witness her in a vulnerable moment but
  she pretends nothing happened. You can press (bad) or let it lie
  (good).
- **Ethan introduction**: at study group / library / coffee shop, you
  meet Ethan as Yuki's "study partner." Polite, sharp, undeniably a
  match-on-paper. Yuki doesn't see him romantically yet — but if MC
  fumbles the Yuki arc, that's where she'd end up.
- **Caleb introduction**: glimpsed in office hours or referenced by
  Mira ("my TA said something interesting…"). Doesn't directly
  confront the MC until Act 3.
- **Tyler at the counter**: ambient presence at Common Grounds in
  every Naia scene. MC notices him after a few visits.

### Act 3 — Complications (weeks 8–10, ~4–6 levels)

Things collide. Choices have consequences.

- **The Halloween Party** (single big multi-character level): everyone
  is there. Marcus is hitting on Emma. Owen shows up looking for June.
  Yuki is uncomfortable but pretending not to be. Mira is on the back
  porch, alone. You have one evening to make calls. This is the
  hinge of the entire game.
- **Backstory unlock**: each girl's deepest backstory becomes available
  in a quiet one-on-one level *after* the Halloween Party, but only if
  her affection is past a threshold.
  - Emma → the ghosting story.
  - Mira → the poetry reading.
  - June → the real Owen story.
  - Yuki → the panic attacks.
  - Naia → the family + money story (delivered at her studio
    apartment, with her on the couch finally not at work).
- **The Owen Confrontation** (June path): Owen finds you alone. He's
  polite. He asks you how serious you are. The wrong answer ends June's
  arc. The right one nearly ends it but doesn't.
- **The June+Yuki Reckoning**: if both their affections are high, an
  event triggers where they have to be in the same room with you. Old
  fight surfaces.

### Act 4 — Resolution (weeks 11–14, ~2–4 levels)

End-of-semester. Choices lock in.

- **The Final Event**: TBD — could be a year-end art show (June's
  showcase finale), a winter formal, a study group all-nighter, a
  Thanksgiving thing. Multiple narrative threads converge.
- **The Choice**: the MC either commits to one girl, walks away
  intentionally, gets walked away from, or fails to commit and ends
  on ambiguity. Each path has a discrete ending scene.

## Endings (sketches)

**Per-girl "together" endings (5):** committed relationship with the
girl in question, with the others having shaped you. Each ending scene
is in a setting specific to her (Emma: coffee at her apartment; Mira:
quiet evening with the notebook open; June: late night in her studio,
calm for the first time; Yuki: her telling her brother, with you in the
room; Naia: a Sunday morning at Common Grounds with the shop closed
and the chairs still on the tables — first time you've been there when
she's not working).

**"Hurt everyone" bad ending:** tried to date multiple, got found out,
all four are gone. Walking home alone in the snow.

**"Walked away" neutral ending:** the MC realizes mid-game they're not
ready and intentionally steps back from all four. Bittersweet, mature.

**"It's complicated" semi-endings:** ambiguous mid-paths where one
girl is "thinking about you" but nothing is settled. Sets up sequel
material.

**Secret "eternal flirt" ending:** get all five to ≥60 affection but
never commit to any. You're invited to all their things forever and
date none of them. Read as either tragedy or comedy depending on
tone.

## Open questions for iteration (v2)

Resolved from v1:
- ✓ Fifth girl: yes — Naia, the social-network hub. Connects to
  everyone through her job.
- ✓ MC personality: stays blank-slate / undefined.
- ✓ More male rivals: yes — Ethan (Yuki), Caleb (Mira), Tyler (Naia)
  added; Marcus + Owen remain.

Still open:
1. **Mental-health tone calibration.** Yuki's panic attacks, June's
   controlling ex, and (now) Naia's financial weight all go into
   territory that needs to feel respectful rather than exploitative.
   Need a writing tone guide before authoring those reveal levels.
2. **"Friendship-only" paths.** Could one of the five end as a *friend*
